How dotLab works

Make this game compiles your words into a playable Three.js slice (no waiting on the big model).

Play starts Vite for that folder and opens a tab.

Craft chips (when a project is open) tweak feel, enemies, palette in milliseconds.

Continue chat can still call the local model for dialogue, systems, hard features.
